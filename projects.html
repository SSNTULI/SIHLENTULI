Radio Highsite Refurbishment

Background

"According to the South African Civil Aviation Authority(SACAA) any communications structure, building or other structure, whether temporary or permanent, which has the potential to endanger aviation in navigable airspace, or has the potential to interfere with the operation of navigation or surveillance systems or Instrument Landing Systems, including meteorological systems for aeronautical purposes, is considered an OBSTACLE and shall be submitted to the Commissioner for Civil Aviation for evaluation.”

To prevent Obstacles that could have a serious effect on aviation safety, especially in the vicinity of an aerodrome marking is required. Any structure exceeding 45m above ground level must be marked.The marking required for the Communication Tower is the day and night marking according to the ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organisation) Annex 14 chapter 14 and SA CAT 39. The marking provides maximum visibility of an obstruction.

The marking condition of the Kimberley Tower was not complying to both the ICAO and SACAA regulation.

Scope

Mark the tower mast in accordance to the ICAO

Trunk Radio High Site Monitoring Backend

Background

Radio communication network plays a significant role in Transnet Freight Rail’s (TFR) daily operation. The main purpose of the network within TFR is for verbal communication between the train driver and the train control officer (TCO). The availability of the network is very critical; hence the stability of the network is constantly monitored at the network operating centre (NOC) to ensure a proactive response to network’s anomalies.TFR’s trunking network consist of 14 nodes, each node is monitored from a separate computer with its own display. The current setup is challenging for the NOC Engineering Technicians (ET) to monitor the network efficiently due to the number of displays that need parallel attention.

Scope

The aim of the project is to consolidate the views of all the nodes into one view (i.e. to view all the nodes in one screen) to increase the monitoring efficiency.

Learning Outcome

  • Python
  • Windows service Application
  • Postgres SQL
